In-Network Insurance

I am in network with:

  • Anthem/Regence BlueCross BlueShield
  • UnitedHealthcare / Optum
  • Aetna
  • Cigna
  • Select Health
  • EMI Health
  • PEHP

I’m not formally in-network with DMBA, but I bill them directly. You simply pay your copay, and DMBA covers the remainder — so the process works much like an in-network claim.


If you’re unsure whether your plan is covered, please reach out — I’m glad to help you check benefits before we begin.

Private Pay Rate

$100 per 60 min session

Private pay sessions are available for those choosing not to use insurance. I accept all major credit cards and HSA/FSA cards through a secure client portal.

Some clients prefer private pay for privacy or flexibility. If you have questions, I’m happy to help you find the simplest path forward.

I can help you confirm your benefits or discuss payment options.
