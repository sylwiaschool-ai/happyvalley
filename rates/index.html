Rates & Insurance

Insurance and payment made simple, so you can focus on healing.

In-Network Insurance

I am in network with the following insurance companies:

  • Regence BlueCross BlueShield
  • UnitedHealthcare / Optum
  • Aetna
  • Cigna
  • Select Health
  • EMI Health
  • PEHP

I’m not formally in network with DMBA, but I’m able to bill them directly. You simply pay your regular copay, and DMBA covers the remainder — so the process works much like an in-network claim.

I am out-of-network with Tricare.

I partner with two trusted billing platforms that process insurance claims securely and make billing as smooth as possible. If you’re unsure whether your plan is covered, please reach out — I’m glad to help you check benefits before we begin.

Private Pay

Private pay sessions are available for those choosing not to use insurance. I accept all major credit cards and HSA/FSA cards through a secure client portal.

Some clients prefer private pay for privacy or flexibility. If you have questions, I’m happy to help you find the simplest path forward.

Not sure about your coverage?

I can help you confirm your benefits or discuss payment options.
